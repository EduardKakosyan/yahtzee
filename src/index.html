          Yahtzee!

          How to play

          The game. 13 rounds; everyone takes one turn each round, in seating order. A turn is up to three rolls. After the first and second roll you may hold any dice — held dice keep their value, tap one again to release it. After one, two or three rolls you must score in exactly one open box, and that score is final.

          Upper section

          Ones to Sixes: add up the dice showing that number. Reach 63 in the six upper boxes for a 35-point bonus.

          Lower section

          • Three of a kind — all five dice added, if at least three match; else 0.
          • Four of a kind — all five dice added, if at least four match; else 0.
          • Full house — three of one number and two of another: 25. Five of a kind is not a full house.
          • Small straight — four in sequence: 30.
          • Large straight — five in sequence: 40.
          • Yahtzee — five of a kind: 50.
          • Chance — the sum of all five dice.

          Yahtzee bonus & the Joker

          Once your Yahtzee box holds 50, every further Yahtzee wins a 100-point bonus (a box holding 0 earns none). The Joker then says where it goes: 1) your upper box for that number if it is still open — mandatory, scored as the sum of the dice; 2) otherwise any open lower box, with full house 25, small straight 30, large straight 40 and the rest the sum of the dice; 3) if that upper box and every lower box are filled, you write 0 in an open upper box of your choice.

          Winning

          Grand total = upper section + 35 bonus + lower section + Yahtzee bonuses. Highest total wins; a tie means everyone tied wins. Play again keeps the same friends, rotates who starts and keeps counting for the evening.
